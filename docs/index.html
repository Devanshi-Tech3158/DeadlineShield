Intelligent Priority Engine

Real-time multi-factor prioritization running completely independent of external APIs.

CRITICAL PRIORITY

Due ≤ 24h or High Workload Pressure

Triggered when a deadline is due today or overdue, or when the effort-to-time ratio exceeds manageable study limits. Requires urgent immediate focus.

HIGH PRIORITY

Due ≤ 48-72h or Hard Difficulty

Examinations and large projects due in 2-3 days. Starting now guarantees avoiding midnight cramming.

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Due in 4 to 7 Days

Normal academic pace. Ideal candidate for the AI Task Planner to allocate 1-2 hours per day.

LOW PRIORITY

Due > 1 Week or Light Effort

Far-off deadlines, optional readings, or easy submissions. Safe to defer while tackling critical tasks.

Multi-Factor Scoring Formula

DeadlineShield uses a deterministic formula combining:

  • Time Remaining: Higher weight as deadline approaches (<12h, <24h, <48h, <72h).
  • Effort-to-Time Ratio: High effort tasks close to deadlines receive emergency boost.
  • Category Multiplier: Examinations (1.5x) and Projects (1.4x) carry higher academic stakes than standard homework.
  • Difficulty Multiplier: Hard (1.4x), Medium (1.1x), Easy (0.9x).
  • Automatic Student Explanations: Every single recommendation comes with human-readable rationale.
